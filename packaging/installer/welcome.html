PatchPrimeMac

A mixing console for your Mac's audio: your microphones and inputs, your apps' sound, mix buses and outputs, with effects on any of them. Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).

This installs:

  • PatchPrimeMac in Applications, with its audio engine, which starts when you log in and runs in the background.
  • An audio driver that adds PatchPrimeMac's virtual devices to the Mac (Patch Desktop, Patch Mic and any you create).

At the end of the install, all sound on the Mac stops for a moment while macOS's audio system restarts with the driver. If your speakers or amplifier are loud, turn them down first. Apps using sound at that moment usually carry on; a few (some Electron apps, such as Signal) may close and need reopening.

Hardware output starts off, so nothing reaches your speakers until you turn it on in the app.
