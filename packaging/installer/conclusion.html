PatchPrimeMac is installed

Open PatchPrimeMac from Applications. Its user guide is in Help → PatchPrimeMac Help.

When macOS asks to let PatchPrimeMac use the microphone, click Allow, or the mics stay silent. It asks again after each update.

To remove it, use Uninstall PatchPrimeMac on the disk image, or Help → Uninstall PatchPrimeMac… in the app.
