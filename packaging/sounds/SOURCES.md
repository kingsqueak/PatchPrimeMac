# The soundboard's built-in sounds

All are 48 kHz 16-bit WAV, levelled to the same loudness (the loudest 400 ms at
−16 LUFS, true peak at most −2 dBTP) by `scripts/make-sounds.py`, which also makes the
synthesized ones. Metadata is stripped.

## Synthesized for PatchPrimeMac

GPL-3.0-or-later, like the rest of PatchPrimeMac (made by `scripts/make-sounds.py`):
Kick, Snare, Clap, Hi-Hat, Crash, Ba-Dum-Tss, Drum Roll, Air Horn, Siren Wail, Siren
Yelp, Sad Trombone.
