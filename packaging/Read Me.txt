PatchPrimeMac
=============

A mixing console for your Mac's audio. Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
https://github.com/kingsqueak/PatchPrimeMac

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 26 or later.

Installing
----------
1. Open PatchPrimeMac-x.y.z.pkg.
2. PatchPrimeMac isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS stops it the
   first time ("Apple cannot check it"). Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click Open Anyway beside PatchPrimeMac, then open the installer
   again. (Or, in Terminal, before opening the disk image:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/PatchPrimeMac-*.dmg )
3. The installer asks for your password: it installs an audio driver.
4. At the end, ALL SOUND ON THE MAC STOPS FOR A MOMENT while macOS's audio restarts
   with the driver. Turn loud speakers or amplifiers down first. A few apps that
   are using sound (some Electron apps, such as Signal) may close; reopen them.
5. Open PatchPrimeMac from Applications. The first time a microphone is used, allow
   PatchPrimeMac to use it.

The user guide is in the app: Help > PatchPrimeMac Help.

Uninstalling
------------
Open "Uninstall PatchPrimeMac" (here, or Help > Uninstall PatchPrimeMac... in the app).
It removes everything PatchPrimeMac added and puts your default sound devices back; you
choose whether to keep your settings, presets and scenes.
